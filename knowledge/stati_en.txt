THE ARTICLE "SAMADHI"
Russian page: quantareon.com/samadhi-ru.html
English page: quantareon.com/samadhi.html
About a state beyond thought: a portal into other dimensions of being,
where fear and duality dissolve. How it is reached — not by an act of
will but by observation, by holding attention in the "zero point" between
thoughts; the article says that holding that point for 28 minutes and 48
seconds brings on Samadhi. Science and spirituality: the brain shifts into
a theta rhythm (7–8 Hz), matching the Schumann resonance. Types of
Samadhi: Savikalpa (the first step, where identification with the ego
dissolves), Nirvikalpa (merging with the Absolute), Sahaja (the highest
state, free of karmic attachment). Full liberation requires clearing the
Sushumna channel, through which the Kundalini energy moves — here that is
the energy/concept, not the book "Kundalini", do not confuse the two. The
article ends with three practical steps: a quiet place, breathing,
observing thoughts without engaging them.
