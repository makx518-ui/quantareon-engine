WHAT IT IS
Your living guide into the world of dreams, numbers, and ancient runes. The
Oracle interprets dreams, performs astrological and numerological calculations,
and casts runes. You can ask it anything — about a dream, a number, a rune,
life, your fate.
Available in 11 languages: English, Russian, Spanish, French, German, Italian,
Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi. Just speak or write — the Oracle
detects your language automatically and replies in it.

HOW TO TALK TO THE ORACLE
In text — just write in the field below.
By voice — press the grey microphone button. It turns red when listening;
words appear in the field in real time; press the red button again to stop.
Need to add more? Press the microphone again and new words are added to what
you said. For best quality use your keyboard microphone (Gboard, iOS dictation,
Samsung Voice) — it adds punctuation automatically.

HOW THE INTERPRETATION ARRIVES
When everything is discussed and you confirm, a window opens with the full
interpretation. While you read, the voice file loads in the background — press
the button and hear it in a living warm voice. (Voice playback is not available
for astrological interpretations.)

SIX DIRECTIONS

1. DREAM INTERPRETATION. Tell what you dreamed; the Oracle asks clarifying
questions (emotions, images, colors) and opens a deep interpretation with
archetypes, symbols and Jungian depth. It also creates a unique map of your
dream: archetypes, symbols and hidden images form a mystical spread.

2. NUMEROLOGY. Send your birth date in the format 30.01.1987. The Oracle
reveals your destiny number, psychomatrix, lines of power, karmic lessons,
analysis by six classical systems, and can give a forecast for any date.

3. RUNE CASTING. Ask your question and the Oracle casts a spread from the Elder
Futhark with an interpretation of each rune and an overall message.

4. BLOOD MYSTERY. Give your blood type with Rh factor and your date of birth.
The Oracle reads the ancient secrets hidden in your blood: true nature, shadow
side, love patterns, life mission, and a secret message from your bloodline.

5. ASTROLOGY — professional chart reading (Expert+). Send country, city, date
and time of birth, and your gender. The more precise the birth time (to the
minute), the more accurate the horoscope. Three types: natal chart (planets,
houses, aspects), compatibility/synastry between two people (both birth dates),
and solar return (year forecast from birthday to birthday).

6. LUCK FORECAST. A unique calculation system based on the wisdom of ages,
using new technologies. Shows which days of the month are favorable for money,
investments and deals: the luck level of each of the 30 days and the exact
hours when it is best to act. For any day you can ask the Oracle for advice.
Send date and time of birth, country and city of birth, gender, and where you
are now.
