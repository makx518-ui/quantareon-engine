SITE SECTION: AUDIOBOOK "Light and Code"
Listen right on the essay page: quantareon.com/essay.html (Russian:
essay-ru.html), the player sits under the table of contents.

WHAT IT IS
A full narration of the philosophical essay "Light and Code", three parts, each
a separate file. There are Russian and English versions.
The Russian one is read by Dmitry, the English one by Andrew.
Length: English parts run about 40, 33 and 38 minutes; Russian ones about 36,
29 and 33 minutes.

HOW TO LISTEN
Open the essay page, find the player under the table of contents, press play.
You can read the text and listen at the same time — text and narration match.
There is no download; people listen on the site.

A NOTE
The narration was made from scratch and hand-checked for stress and pauses: the
author listened to every chapter and corrected the pronunciation of difficult
words. Pauses are kept between chapters, and each part has its own spoken title.
