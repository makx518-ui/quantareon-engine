HOW IT WORKS
The horoscope is built from the second the visitor entered the site. That
second becomes the Ascendant of the chart, and the whole pattern of the sky
at that moment unfolds from it: houses by the Placidus system, planets,
aspects, degrees and their meanings. The machine does the calculation, to
the second; an advanced artificial-intelligence model writes the living
interpretation. It is the same second as the free horoscope of the day on
the home page: one chart, read deeper.

FOUR HOROSCOPES
- Daily — the full reading of the current day: the core of the day, the
  karmic points, the planetary pattern by houses and hours, the nerve and
  the summary. Ready in about five minutes.
- Weekly — the karmic vector of the week, the core, each of the seven days
  hour by hour, the nerve of the week and the point of exit with advice.
  About five minutes.
- Monthly — four weeks, each with all its days and a summary of the week,
  then the nerve and the point of exit of the month. Up to ten minutes.
- Yearly — the karmic vector, the core and an overview of the year, twelve
  months linked by bridges, and at the end the scenario of the year as one
  story. About thirty minutes.
The daily one is a snapshot — here and now. A week, a month and a year are a
story: every day has a yesterday and a tomorrow. They are not stitched from
separate days: the machine unfolds the whole period from the one second of
entry at once, and it reads as one story from the point of entry to the
point of exit.
