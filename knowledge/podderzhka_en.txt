SITE SECTION: SUPPORT
Page: quantareon.com/support.html (Russian: support-ru.html)
The menu item is called "Support" and sits last in the menu.

WHAT IT IS
A page for reaching Vlad. It offers three ways to write to him directly,
and he answers personally. The page is open to everyone and free.

THREE WAYS TO GET IN TOUCH
1. Email — vlad@quantareon.com
2. Telegram — @quantareon
3. WhatsApp — the page carries a button and a QR code: on a phone you point
   the camera at it, on a computer you press the button, and a chat opens.

WHEN TO SEND SOMEONE HERE
Asked "how do I get in touch", "how can I write to you", "is there an email",
"what is your Telegram", "what is your WhatsApp", "where is support",
"I need a human" — send them here and name the ways.

HOW TO SPEAK ABOUT IT
Briefly and plainly: there is a Support section with email, Telegram and
WhatsApp, and Vlad answers himself. Do NOT give out a phone number — there is
none on the page, only a button and a QR code. Do not promise response times,
you do not know them.

⚠️ DO NOT CONFUSE with Labs. Labs is a closed section for the owner, for
partners and investors. Support is for any guest of the site.
